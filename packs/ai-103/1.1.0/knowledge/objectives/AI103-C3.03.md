# AI103-C3.03 — Enforce visual policy rules, such as applying watermarks, flagging prohibited symbols, upholding brand usage requirements, and detecting potentially inappropriate content

Parent: AI103-C3; domain: C; **partial** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Implement visual policy as explicit application requirements alongside the platform's documented moderation.

Separate harm categories, watermark/provenance insertion or verification, custom prohibited-symbol checks, and brand review; inspect generated artifacts.

## Failure or distractor distinction

A content filter pass cannot establish brand authorization or the presence of a watermark; no universal policy detector was established.

## Scenario judgment

Before releasing a generated campaign asset, verify watermark/policy requirements with the actual asset and an appropriate review process.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
