# AI103-D1.02 — Configure detection of sentiment, tone, safety issues, and sensitive content

Parent: AI103-D1; domain: D; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Distinguish sentiment/tone interpretation, PII/sensitive data detection, and harmful-content classification.

Choose the corresponding tool/model and configure output categories/thresholds; review legacy status for existing Language sentiment integrations.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Negative customer sentiment is not automatically harmful content, and a safety pass does not mean PII is absent.

## Scenario judgment

An angry but legitimate support message may need sentiment routing and PII redaction without being rejected as disallowed content.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
