# AI103-D2.03 — Enable multimodal reasoning from audio inputs

Parent: AI103-D2; domain: D; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Use a supported audio-capable conversational model/interface when reasoning should operate on audio interaction, and distinguish it from transcript-only processing.

Configure audio session inputs/outputs, VAD/turn rules, interruptions, and any permitted additional modalities.

## Failure or distractor distinction

A transcription service produces text but does not itself establish general multimodal reasoning capability.

## Scenario judgment

For a conversational assistant reacting in real time, evaluate its audio turn behavior and reasoning output together.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
