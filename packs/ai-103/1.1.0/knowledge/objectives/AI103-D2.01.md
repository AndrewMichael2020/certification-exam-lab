# AI103-D2.01 — Implement workflows to convert speech to text and text to speech for agentic interactions

Parent: AI103-D2; domain: D; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Choose STT/TTS components for a controlled speech pipeline or Voice Live for a managed conversational integration.

Configure recognition language/input, synthesis voice/output, turn handling, and cancellation; check service/model/region compatibility.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Recognized text and synthesized speech are separate artifacts; voice turns must account for user interruption.

## Scenario judgment

A voice support agent needs a clear stop/interrupt behavior so stale speech does not continue after the user changes the task.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
