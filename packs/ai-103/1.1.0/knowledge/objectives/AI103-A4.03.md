# AI103-A4.03 — Implement auditing through trace logging, provenance metadata, and approval workflows

Parent: AI103-A4; domain: A; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Tie operational traces to evidence provenance and explicit approvals for actions with side effects.

Retain trace/evaluation IDs, content source locators, tool-call decisions, and approval records while redacting secrets.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Logging full prompts can expose sensitive material without proving where an answer came from.

## Scenario judgment

A document-based approval should preserve the source clause/location and approval event; telemetry access needs the same controls as production logs.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
