# AI103-A3.03 — Monitor data ingestion quality, search index health, and relevance performance

Parent: AI103-A3; domain: A; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Monitor ingestion/indexer failures and service health separately from search relevance.

Inspect execution history and item warnings/errors; configure latency/throttle/storage alerts; evaluate retrieval against relevance labels.

## Failure or distractor distinction

A partially successful indexer can leave required documents absent; index health does not establish relevance.

## Scenario judgment

If questions about recently ingested documents fail, inspect indexer errors/counts before retuning the LLM.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
