# AI103-D2.02 — Integrate speech as an agent modality, including custom speech models

Parent: AI103-D2; domain: D; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Use Custom Speech to improve recognition of domain terms and audio conditions, and integrate recognition into the chosen agent modality.

Compare base/custom recognition, train with suitable data, deploy a custom endpoint, and confirm the selected conversational product supports that customization.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Custom Speech recognition is different from custom voice synthesis; one model cannot be substituted for the other.

## Scenario judgment

A warehouse agent struggling with product codes needs recognition customization evaluated against transcription errors before changing the answer model.
