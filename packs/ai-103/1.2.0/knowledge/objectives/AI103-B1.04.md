# AI103-B1.04 — Evaluate models and apps, including detecting fabrications, relevance, quality, and safety

Parent: AI103-B1; domain: B; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Evaluate generated responses, retrieved context, and tool behavior with different measures.

Choose ground-truth retrieval metrics when labels exist; otherwise treat an LLM judge as a fallible evaluator; inspect errors and compare versions.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Groundedness can be high for an incomplete answer; relevance can be high for unsupported detail.

## Scenario judgment

A support answer that repeats only one of three required remedies needs completeness review even if its stated remedy is grounded.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
