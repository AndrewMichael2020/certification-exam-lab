# AI103-C2.04 — Configure generation of alt‑text and extended image descriptions aligned to accessibility guidelines

Parent: AI103-C2; domain: C; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Generate descriptions according to the image's purpose in the page: informative, functional, decorative, or complex.

Use current visual models for draft descriptions; apply W3C purpose/context rules, provide extended detail for complex information, and review wording.

## Failure or distractor distinction

A generic caption is not an accessibility guarantee; a search-icon button needs its action described and a decorative image may need empty alt.

## Scenario judgment

For an image-only download button, communicate the download action; for a chart, provide the meaningful data beyond a short caption.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
