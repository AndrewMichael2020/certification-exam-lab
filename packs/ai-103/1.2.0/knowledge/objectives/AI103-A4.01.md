# AI103-A4.01 — Configure safety filters, guardrails, risk detection, and content moderation

Parent: AI103-A4; domain: A; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Apply safety controls at the relevant input/tool/output boundary; distinguish content harm from prompt injection.

Configure risk, severity, intervention point, and response action; preserve agent/Spotlighting preview and API conditions. S015 has conflicting most/least-restrictive labels beside flagging ranges: use explicit severity levels and configured action; avoid those ambiguous adjectives.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Filtering only final text leaves malicious tool-return instructions and dangerous proposed tool arguments unexamined.

## Scenario judgment

An agent reading external pages needs document-attack protection and tool-access constraints in addition to output moderation.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
