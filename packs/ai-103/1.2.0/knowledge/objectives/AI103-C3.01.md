# AI103-C3.01 — Implement filters to classify unsafe or disallowed visual content

Parent: AI103-C3; domain: C; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Use image or multimodal Content Safety harm classification, then apply application thresholds/actions.

Inspect hate/self-harm/sexual/violence severities; choose appropriate reject/review handling; evaluate false positives and negatives for the workload.

## Failure or distractor distinction

Safety labels do not automatically enforce custom brand, trademark, symbol, or watermark rules.

## Scenario judgment

A moderation service should route the returned severity according to explicit policy, rather than treating every nonzero value identically.

Authored inference from cited documented facts; not a reproduced or newly authored exam item.
