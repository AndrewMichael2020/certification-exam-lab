# AI103-C2.06 — Implement video analysis workflows to process and interpret video segments

Parent: AI103-C2; domain: C; **ready** for bounded authoring. Official wording preserved. Documentation examples were not executed.

## Choice and configuration

Use video extraction and configurable segmentation when analysis depends on events over time.

Configure whole-video or custom segments, preserve timestamps/transcripts/keyframes, and check model cost and preview limits.

## Failure or distractor distinction

At roughly one sampled frame per second, a single-frame incident can be missed; lower-detail frames may hide small text.

## Scenario judgment

For a news recording, define story boundaries and keep time ranges so a summary can be checked against the clip.
